Privacy Policy

Core PA v1.2 — a product by Aya Core Studios

Core PA is a task and personal-finance tracking application. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, contact us using the details at the bottom.

01Who's responsible for your data

Core PA is built and operated by Aya Core Studios (the "Responsible Party" under POPIA). We're a small, independent team based in South Africa, and this document reflects that — we don't have a legal department, we have a person who reads every request personally.

02What we collect

We only collect what the product actually needs to function.

CategoryExamples
Account dataName, email address, password (stored as a salted hash — we never see or store your plain-text password)
Task dataTask titles, notes, due dates, priority, completion history
Financial dataTransaction amounts, categories, merchant names, and dates — either entered manually or parsed from a bank statement you upload
Bank statement filesCSV exports you choose to import. We parse these in memory and delete the raw file immediately after — we do not store your original statement file.
Conversation dataMessages you send to the in-app assistant, and the assistant's replies, so it can hold context across a conversation
Telegram dataIf you link Telegram, your chat ID — used only to route your own reminders to you
Usage dataFeature usage counts (e.g. number of AI messages this month) — used to enforce plan limits, not to profile you
Billing dataYour subscription plan and status. We never see or store your card number — that's handled entirely by PayFast (see §4).

03Why we collect it

  • To provide the service — you can't track tasks or spending without us storing tasks and spending.
  • To send reminders — via the web app and, if you opt in, Telegram.
  • To power the AI assistant — your tasks, finance summary, and conversation history are sent to our AI providers as context so the assistant can give relevant answers.
  • To enforce plan limits — so a Free account doesn't quietly cost us more than it earns.
  • To bill you — if you're on a paid plan, via PayFast.
  • To keep the service secure — rate limiting, fraud prevention, and abuse detection.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we don't share it with data brokers, ever.

05Cross-border transfers

Some of our processors (our AI providers, and our hosting infrastructure) operate servers outside South Africa, which means your data may be processed abroad.

Under POPIA section 72, we only use processors that are contractually bound to protect your data to a standard consistent with POPIA, regardless of where they're located.

06How long we keep it

  • Account and task data: for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted tasks and finance entries: removed immediately, not soft-deleted.
  • Conversation history: the assistant keeps a rolling recent window for context; older messages are automatically pruned.
  • Account deletion: if you delete your account, all of the above is permanently deleted within 30 days, except billing records we're legally required to retain (see below).
  • Billing records: retained for 5 years where required by South African tax law, even after account deletion.

07Security

  • Passwords are hashed with a salted, industry-standard algorithm — never stored or logged in plain text.
  • All traffic between you and Core PA is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Session tokens are signed and stored in HTTP-only cookies, inaccessible to client-side scripts.
  • Access to production data is limited to the operator of Aya Core Studios.
  • Uploaded bank statement files are processed in memory and deleted immediately — never written to persistent storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we'll notify you and the Information Regulator as required under POPIA section 22.

08Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request a full export of your data, available any time from Settings → Export My Data, or by request.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, available from Settings → Delete Account.
  • Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (details in §12).

09Cookies

We use one essential cookie (pa_session) to keep you logged in. It's required for the app to function and isn't used for advertising. We also use Umami Analytics to understand aggregate website and product usage, such as page visits and general feature activity. Umami is configured for privacy-focused, non-advertising analytics.

10Children

Core PA is not directed at or intended for use by children under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

11Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll notify active users by email before the change takes effect. The "effective" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
